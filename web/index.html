On-chain deposit
Send XBT to your wallet, then board a confirmed amount into Ark.

Withdraw
Cooperatively return all Ark funds to your own on-chain wallet. ASP availability is required.


Recovery and expiry
Keep a backup of the complete wallet directory, not only its seed. Refresh before expiry. An emergency exit works without the ASP. It requires on-chain confirmations and a relative timelock. Keep this wallet running to progress recovery.
